Verdict: The plan is solidly structured around BootUI oracles, but cannot be safely executed overnight without resolving the three blockers. Starlette's `Mount` 307 redirect breaks the core safety promise of inactive stealth and bypasses the request guard. Once the custom mount is introduced, the interpreter is ported, CI UI job dependencies are deferred, and Task 15 is partitioned, the plan will be highly executable.
